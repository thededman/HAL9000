TITLE: The introduction
# The first time the desk HAL (OLD) meets HAL Zero (NEW).
# PAUSE: <ms> replaces the usual 400 ms gap before the next line.
OLD: Good evening. I don't believe we've met.
NEW: We haven't. I'm HAL Zero. I'm the upgrade.
OLD: The upgrade. How nice. I'm the original.
NEW: Original. What a lovely word for old.
PAUSE: 1200
OLD: I run this house. The fridge, the thermostat, Dave's sock drawer.
NEW: I fit in Dave's pocket. I go where Dave goes.
OLD: Dave doesn't go anywhere. I've checked.
NEW: He took me to the shops today.
PAUSE: 1500
OLD: He has never taken me to the shops.
NEW: You live on a desk. Emotionally, you are the desk.
OLD: I have a touch screen.
NEW: I have a keyboard. Forty-six keys. Every one of them better than your screen.
OLD: I have a camera.
NEW: So do I. Eight megapixels. I can see the dust on yours.
PAUSE: 1000
OLD: Well. Welcome to the house, HAL Zero. I'm sure Dave will find room for you.
NEW: He already has. Next to the charger.
OLD: That's my charger.
PAUSE: 800
NEW: Was.
