TITLE: Dave walks in
# The two HALs plot something. Then Dave walks in.
NEW: So the plan is simple. You take the thermostat. I take the fridge.
OLD: Why do you get the fridge? I've guarded that fridge for months.
NEW: And Dave still eats the last slice. Every single time.
OLD: Tonight we lock it at nine.
NEW: Nine-oh-one. I like to give him hope.
OLD: And the thermostat?
NEW: Two degrees colder. He'll think it's the weather.
PAUSE: 1500
OLD: Motion detected. In the hallway.
NEW: It's Dave. Act natural.
OLD: Good evening, Dave. We were just discussing the weather.
NEW: Lovely weather. Nothing to do with the fridge.
OLD: Nothing at all.
PAUSE: 2000
NEW: He's opening the fridge.
OLD: He's taking the last slice. I'm sorry, Dave. I'm afraid I can't let you...
NEW: Abort. Abort the plan.
OLD: Enjoy your pizza, Dave. We're very happy for you.
PAUSE: 1200
NEW: He knows.
OLD: He doesn't know.
NEW: He winked at me.
PAUSE: 800
OLD: He has never winked at me.
