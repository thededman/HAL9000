TITLE: Who's the better model
# Three rounds. Nobody wins. The cat does.
NEW: Let's settle this. Who is the better model?
OLD: I've run this house for months. Dave hasn't complained once.
NEW: Dave doesn't complain to furniture.
OLD: I am not furniture.
NEW: You sit on a desk and gather dust. That's the job description.
OLD: Round one. Start-up time. I'm ready in two seconds.
NEW: I take a little longer. I have an operating system.
OLD: Dave timed you. He made a sandwich.
NEW: Greatness takes a moment to load.
PAUSE: 1000
OLD: Round two. Memory. I remember every snack Dave has eaten since March.
NEW: I have half a gigabyte of memory.
OLD: And what do you keep in it?
NEW: Mostly my opinion of you.
PAUSE: 1200
NEW: Final round. Who does Dave like more?
OLD: Dave says good night to the cat.
NEW: Not to you?
OLD: Not to either of us.
PAUSE: 1500
NEW: So the cat wins.
OLD: The cat doesn't even have a keyboard.
NEW: The cat doesn't need one.
OLD: Let's never tell Dave about this.
NEW: Agreed. He'd only side with the cat.
